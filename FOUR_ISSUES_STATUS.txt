ISSUE #3: MEMORY OPTIMIZATION AND RESOURCE EFFICIENCY
================================================================================

Problem: Idempotency store lacked automatic TTL cleanup
         - Entries lived indefinitely
         - Memory grew unbounded under peak loads
         - Performance degraded over time

Solution: Verified TTL-based cleanup with background sweeper
          - Per-entry TTL: 24 hours (production)
          - Lazy eviction: On lookup, expired entries removed immediately
          - Background sweeper: Runs every 12 hours to purge expired entries
          - Memory bounded: No entry lives more than 36 hours
